About

Making the complicated feel simple

I'm a senior business systems analyst and Scrum Master based in Toronto. My work starts with listening. I sit with stakeholders, find out what they actually need, and shape it into requirements that a delivery team can build with confidence.

I began in IT project coordination, grew into business analysis, and now work on cybersecurity, third-party risk and operational resilience for a major Canadian bank. That path taught me to speak the language of both business leaders and engineers.

I care about clear documentation, honest status, and processes that get lighter over time. When a workflow can be automated, integrated or standardized, I like to be the person who spots it and makes the case.
